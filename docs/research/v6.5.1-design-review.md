# OpenEVSE PLUS v6.5.1: build parts list and enhancement review

Date: 2026-10-25.

Scope: v6.5.1 only.
- 2-layer EAGLE design: `OpenEVSE_PLUS_v6.5.1/`.
- 6-layer KiCad design: `OpenEVSE_PLUS_v6.5.1_6Layer/`.

Companion files in this folder:

- [`v6.5.1-board-bom.csv`](v6.5.1-board-bom.csv): grouped board BOM extracted from the EAGLE schematic (55 lines). Use the `value` column. The `eagle_device` and `attributes` columns are often leftover library placeholders.
- [`v6.5.1-sourcing-research.md`](v6.5.1-sourcing-research.md):
  - §1: station-level parts and connector mates.
  - §2: datasheet checks.
  - §3: corrected MPNs.
  - §4: sources.

  Bracketed keys such as [MW] or [GUIDE] resolve in its §4.
- [`scripts/`](scripts/): the BOM extractor, the netlist comparator, the KiCad mains-spacing rules and the ngspice pilot testbench used below.

## Top actions

1. **Fix the BOM values that would produce a dead or unsafe board if ordered as written** (§1.2). These are IC1, U1, PS1, D7–D10, C29/C33/C32 and R32–R39.
2. **Bring the J1772 pilot up to spec.** The simulated state-A level is +10.45 V, not +12 V. State B drops to 7.96 V at the supply's low tolerance, which is below the 8 V band (§2.5, §3.1.3).
3. **Resolve the 277 V claim.** The mains connector (JST VH, 250 V), D7–D10 (400 V) and the unrated MOVs don't support it (§3.1.2).
4. **Increase mains-to-low-voltage spacing and make DRC enforce it.** Both layouts have 3.78 mm between line and +5V copper, and 3.9 mm from a mains trace to a mounting hole (§3.1.1).
5. **Pick one meaning for serial-header pin 2.** It is +12 V on the 2-layer board, +5 V on the 6-layer board and "N/C" on the drawing (§3.2.1).
6. **Make KiCad the single, consistent source.** EAGLE is end-of-life, and the 6-layer KiCad project differs from the 2-layer design and from its own schematic (§3.4.1).

### 1.2 Controller board lines that are wrong as written

Each of these was checked against the netlist and the manufacturer datasheets (research §2–§3).

| Ref | Schematic says | Problem | Order instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| IC1 | LMV358 | Its absolute maximum supply is 5.5 V, but this IC runs on ±12 V. It is the pilot driver. | **Open:** the fitted part is unknown. LF353/TL082-class parts work but fall short of ±12 V (§2.5). TLV1805 is the proposed redesign (§3.1.3). |
| U1 | AP63203 | Fixed 3.3 V output. FB ties straight to "+5V", so the rail would be 3.3 V, below the 4.5 V the ATmega328P needs at 16 MHz. | **AP63205WU-7.** The chip marking T5 = 5 V and T4 = 3.3 V, so you can check a board. |
| PS1 | IRM-10-5 | V+ is on the +12V net, and the board photo shows an IRM-10-12 | **Mean Well IRM-10-12** |
| D7–D10 | 1N4004 | That is a DO-41 part, while the footprint is SMA and its library device is MBRA340T3 (a **40 V Schottky**). 400 V is also below the 431 V peak at 277 V +10 %. | **S1M-13-F** (1000 V) or S1J-13-F |
| C29, C33 | "22" (device says 1.0 µF) | These are the buck output caps | 22 µF 16 V X5R 0805, e.g. TDK C2012X5R1C226K125AC |
| C32 | "100nf" (device says 1.0 µF) | Bootstrap cap | 100 nF 50 V X7R 0805 |
| R32–R39 | 180k (device says 0.1 Ω) | Mains-sensing resistors | Yageo RC1210FR-07180KL |
| IC2 | MPN attribute ATMEGA8-16AU | Leftover library attribute | ATmega328P-AU |
| IC8 | HCPL-2731S | The onsemi part is obsolete | Broadcom HCPL-2731-500E (gull-wing). Check the lead span against the 10.16 mm pad rows. |
| C17 | 10 µF 6.3 V tantalum | Runs at 79 % of rated voltage on 5 V, against KEMET's 50 % guidance | A ≥10 V tantalum, or an MLCC |
| C5, C6, C13, C28 | 10 µF 0805, no voltage given | On ±12 V, a 25 V X5R part keeps only about 1.7 µF at 12.5 V | ≥25 V. Consider 1206/1210. |
| M1–M4 | "MOV" | No voltage or energy rating | **Open.** Littelfuse V20E320P (320 VAC) fits the footprint. |

The root cause is library reuse. Devicesets and PROD_ID/MPN attributes come from unrelated SparkFun/Atmel parts, and only the `value` text was edited. A BOM generated from the design files inherits every mismatch.

### 2.5 Pilot generator simulation

The testbench models IC1 used open-loop as a comparator:
- **Input:** +IN is the 0/5 V 1 kHz MCU PWM, and −IN is biased to 2.5 V.
- **Supplies:** +12 V, and −12 V from the TC1044S through 70 Ω.
- **Pilot path:** R1 = 820 Ω, plus the R5/R6/R7 PILOTREAD divider.
- **EV loads:** a diode plus 2.74 kΩ (B), 882 Ω (C) or 246 Ω (D).
- **Reference row:** an ideal ±12 V source behind 1 kΩ. It reproduces the J1772 nominal values (9.0, 6.0, 3.0 V), which validates the bench.

Rise and fall times are 10–90 % at the amplifier output; J1772 requires less than 2 µs [TI-FE6]. Bands are B 8–10 V, C 5–7 V and D 2–4 V [TI-FE6]. The state-A tolerance of ±0.6 V comes from SAE J1772, which is paywalled, so that figure is unverified.

| Rails | Driver | A high | A low | B | C | D | Rise / fall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ±12.0 V | LF353 / TL082 | **10.45** | −10.3 / −9.7 | 8.20 | 5.75 | 2.95 | 1.3 / 1.3 µs |
| ±12.0 V | LM358 | **10.80** | −11.9 | 8.35 | 5.81 | 2.96 | **63 / 180 µs** |
| ±12.0 V | LM358B | **10.80** | −11.9 | 8.35 | 5.81 | 2.96 | **29 / 29 µs** |
| ±12.0 V | ideal, 1 kΩ | 11.96 | −11.94 | 8.94 | 5.97 | 2.92 | – |
| ±11.7 V (IRM −2.5 %) | LF353 / TL082 | **10.15** | −10.0 / −9.4 | **7.96** | 5.59 | 2.88 | 1.3 / 1.2 µs |

What the results show:

- **Bipolar op-amps (LM358, LM358B) are far too slow** for the J1772 edge requirement.
- **JFET op-amps (LF353, TL082) meet the edge requirement but not the amplitude.** They lose about 1.5 V of headroom on each rail, and the 820 Ω R1 only partly compensates: the effective source resistance is about 830 Ω against the specified 1 kΩ.
- **State B has almost no margin.** At the IRM-10-12's −2.5 % limit it falls below the 8 V band edge.

## 3. Enhancements

Priority labels: **P1** is safety or correctness, **P2** is reliability or usability, **P3** is polish. Unless noted, each item applies to both v6.5.1 layouts.

### 3.1 Safety and longevity

**3.1.1 Mains spacing (P1).** Measured with KiCad DRC on both layouts; the closest pairs are identical:

| Pair | Distance |
|---|---|
| ACLIN (IC7 pin 8) ↔ +5V (IC8 pin 8) | 3.78 mm clearance |
| ACLIN ↔ GMI_LINE (IC8 pin 7) | 5.74 mm |
| ACTEST1 trace ↔ edge of the non-plated top-right mounting hole | 3.91 mm creepage |

That mounting hole has no keepout, so the face of a 4.5 mm hex standoff takes off up to about another 1 mm.

How these compare with the free IEC 60664-1 / 62368-1 excerpts (research 2.17):

| Requirement | Limit | 3.78 mm |
|---|---|---|
| Basic clearance, 60664-1, OVC III | 3.0 mm | Passes |
| Basic clearance, 62368-1 | 3.8 mm | Fails, barely |
| Basic creepage, material group III, 400 V row | 4.0 mm | Fails |
| Reinforced: 5.5 mm clearance, 8.0 mm creepage | – | Far off |

Basic insulation is enough only if the low-voltage side is earthed (3.1.5).

Neither design has slots or a mains net class: the EAGLE rules are a uniform 6 mil and the KiCad default is 0.15 mm, so DRC can't catch this.

*Change:*
- Separate IC7's mains pins from IC8's output side, or route a slot between them.
- Add a keepout around the top-right hole, or reroute ACTEST1.
- Add a mains net class with clearance and creepage constraints (KiCad 10 supports `creepage`). `scripts/mains-selv-report.kicad_dru` is a starting point.
- For outdoor enclosures, specify conformal coating.

**3.1.2 277 V input (P1).** The README claims 277 VAC, but:

- The JST VH connector carrying L, N, coil and test is rated 250 V AC/DC.
- D7–D10 (400 V) see a 431 V peak at 277 V +10 %.
- The MOVs are unrated.
- The kit range is 208–264 VAC, and the C240C coil is rated 208–240 VAC.

*Change:* either rate v6.5.1 at 240 V nominal (264 V max), or for 277 V:
- Use a ≥300 V connector. Molex KK 396 is rated 600 V at the same 3.96 mm pitch, but it doesn't mate with VH, and its footprint needs checking.
- Fit S1M diodes.
- Fit 320 VAC MOVs.

**3.1.3 Pilot amplitude (P1).** See §2.5. TI's J1772 reference design (TIDA-010939, [TI-FE6] §2.2.1.4) drives the pilot with a **TLV1805**. That is a rail-to-rail push-pull comparator rated ±18 V with more than 100 mA peak output, used behind 1 kΩ.

*Change:*
- Use a TLV1805-class driver and set R1 to 1 kΩ. Expect the "ideal 1 kΩ" row of the table. The pinout differs from the current dual op-amp, so this is a layout change.
- Revisit the −12 V source too. The TC1044S's operating maximum is 12 V (13 V absolute), and the IRM can sit at 12.3 V (research 2.6).

**3.1.4 Fusing (P2).** F1 protects only PS1. ACLIN reaches IC7, which switches the contactor coil, and the L–N MOV M2 and L–PE MOV M3, all without a fuse. A shorted coil lead, or a MOV that fails short at end of life, is cleared only by the 40–60 A branch breaker, after the board's traces and IC7 have failed.

*Change:* fuse the coil circuit. Put the MOVs behind a fuse, or use thermally protected MOVs. TMOV20RP320E needs 7.5 mm lead spacing, so that is a footprint change.

**3.1.5 GND–PE bond (P2).** Board GND (G2, mounting hole U$9) and PE (G1, U$3) are separate nets. They are joined only through the mounting hardware and plate, or a wire [ADV24]. Both the pilot's reference and the insulation grade in 3.1.1 depend on that joint, and a corroded standoff degrades it silently.

*Change:* a dedicated, documented bond, either an on-board link or a ring-lug wire. **Confirm** that the ground-monitor design doesn't rely on keeping the two separate.

**3.1.6 Ground monitor margin on 240 V split-phase (P2).** With 2× 180 kΩ per branch, a 120 V line-to-earth leg drives the HCPL-2731 LED at about 0.47 mA peak. That is below the 0.5 mA point where its CTR is guaranteed (research 2.10). That is the most common US service.

*Change (calc, not yet validated):* 2× 100 kΩ gives about 0.84 mA at 120 V. At 305 V it gives about 2.2 mA peak and about 0.12 W per 1210 resistor, against a ½ W rating. Check open_evse's ground-monitor/AC-detect sampling before changing the pulse width.

**3.1.7 Derating (P2).**
- C17: see §1.2.
- C22 is polarized on +12 V with no voltage rating.
- The 10 µF 0805 parts lose about 83 % of their capacitance at 12 V.
- The IRM-10-12 delivers full load only up to 50 °C ambient (fine at this board's load, but worth stating for enclosure design).
- The AQH2223 triac output derates from 0.9 A to about 0.3 A at 85 °C, and the C240C coil current is unpublished.

*Change:* give voltage ratings on every capacitor. Measure the coil current and confirm the AQH2223's margin over the enclosure temperature range.

### 3.2 End users and installers

**3.2.1 Serial header pin 2 (P1).** The header pinout is GND / pin 2 / +5V / RX / TX / RESET:

| Source | Pin 2 | Pin 6 |
|---|---|---|
| 2-layer board | +12 V | RESET |
| 6-layer board | +5 V | RESET |
| v6.5 drawing | N/C | N/C |

The LCD/WiFi module accepts either voltage on pin 2 (input "+5v–16v DC"). However, common 6-pin USB-serial adapters put CTS on pin 2, so an adapter wired to the drawing receives 12 V on the 2-layer board.

*Change:* choose one voltage for both layouts and put it on the silkscreen and the drawing.

**3.2.2 Pin legends (P2).** The silkscreen names connectors but not pins. Only pin 1 of AC is marked, although its six identical-looking pins carry line, neutral, contactor coil and AC test. Pinouts exist only in the Visio drawing.

*Change:* add per-pin legends (AC IN 1/2, COIL, TEST 1/2, CT pins, PILOT/PP) and a short URL to the wiring drawing.

**3.2.3 Status LEDs (P2).** The board has no LEDs, so power and MCU health are visible only through the display module.

*Change:*
- Add a +5 V power LED; it needs no MCU pin.
- Apart from the ISP lines (PB3–PB5, which go only to the ISP header), every digital MCU pin is in use. A firmware status LED could share SCK (PB5) through a high-value resistor, as Arduino's pin-13 LED does, so ISP programming still works. Alternatively, put it on an I2C expander.

**3.2.4 Station wiring drawing for v6.5.1 (P2).** The official guide points at the v5.5 and v5 drawings.

*Change:* publish a v6.5.1 station drawing that covers the contactor, CTs, ground bar and bonding (3.1.5).

### 3.3 Builders and tinkerers

**3.3.1 BOM with real MPNs (P1).** Fix the library devices or attributes so an exported BOM is orderable. Start from §1.2 and research §3, and add Manufacturer/MPN fields per part.

**3.3.2 Release outputs (P2).**
- The v6.5.1 2-layer directory has no Gerbers, drill files, BOM, pick-and-place (CPL) file or schematic PDF, only the Visio drawing and a photo.
- The 6-layer directory has Gerbers and drill files, but no BOM, CPL or PDF.

*Change:* each revision should ship Gerbers + drill, BOM, CPL, schematic PDF and an assembly drawing, ideally generated by CI (3.4.2).

**3.3.3 Polarity marks (P2).** The six SOD123 diodes (D2–D6, D12) have no silkscreen polarity mark on either layout. The EAGLE footprint draws the cathode bar only on the non-printed tDocu layer, and the KiCad import has no silkscreen for them at all.

*Change:* add a cathode bar on the silkscreen.

**3.3.4 Test points (P3).** The board has none.

*Change:* add labelled pads for +12 V, −12 V, +5 V, GND, PILOT, the GFCI CT signal and AMP_READ, for bring-up and production test.

**3.3.5 Assembly sides (P3).** All SMD parts are on the top. All through-hole parts (connectors, PSU, MOVs, fuse, ISP header) are mounted from the bottom, so their joints land on the SMD side, and through-hole parts must be hand- or selectively soldered after reflow. This keeps the SMD side facing the mounting plate; the trade-off should be decided consciously if the board moves to contract assembly.

### 3.4 Developers

**3.4.1 One consistent KiCad source (P1).** Autodesk ended EAGLE in June 2026 ([Autodesk](https://www.autodesk.com/support/technical/article/caas/sfdcarticles/sfdcarticles/Eagle-can-be-used-after-the-end-of-life.html)). The 6-layer KiCad project is the start of the migration, but:

- **It is not electrically the 2-layer design.**
  - Serial pin 2 is +5 V instead of +12 V.
  - JP2 pin 2 is unconnected instead of GND.
  - C9 (+5 V decoupling) and C28 (+12 V, 10 µF) are gone.
  - XGND and UGND are merged into GND (the net-tie shorts SH1/SH2 were dropped).
- **Its schematic and board disagree.**
  - U1 FB is unconnected in the schematic but on +5V on the board.
  - C9 is in the schematic but not on the board.
  - Six references differ (`FTDI0`/`FTDI`, `I2C0`/`I2C`, `PILOT0`/`PILOT`, `TVS0`/`TVS`, `GFCI_CT0`/`GFCI_CT`, `DC_RELAY0`/`DC_RELAY`). "Update PCB from Schematic" would remove and re-add those footprints.
- **Its libraries are missing.** The `OpenEVSE_PLUS_v6.5RC2` symbol/footprint libraries aren't in the repo, which causes 167 ERC and 109 DRC issues. Per-user files (`.kicad_prl`, `fp-info-cache`) are committed.

The 2-layer board imports cleanly with `kicad-cli pcb import --format eagle`: 117 footprints, 686 tracks, 63 vias. Twenty graphics on unmapped layers needed remapping.

*Change:*
- Decide whether the 2-layer board gets a KiCad port.
- Reconcile the differences above, each as an explicit design decision.
- Commit the project libraries.
- Add a KiCad `.gitignore`.

**3.4.2 CI (P2).** `kicad-cli` (10.x) can run ERC and DRC, including the mains rules, and export Gerbers, BOM, CPL and PDF on every tag, which gives builders the files in 3.3.2 without manual steps. The scripts in `scripts/` show the commands.

**3.4.3 Board ID (P2).** Each new board feature currently uses a dedicated strap pin: PD7 marks v5.5+, and ADC6 marks continuous ground monitoring. ADC7 is unconnected. A resistor divider on ADC7 could encode many future board revisions on one pin; the digital pins are nearly exhausted (3.2.3). The I2C header is the expansion path for new peripherals.

**3.4.4 Header pinouts in the repo (P3).** The only pinout reference is a Visio PDF without its source file, and it disagrees with the netlist on serial pins 2 and 6.

*Change:* keep a text pinout table next to the design files, generated from the netlist or at least checked against it.

## 4. Open questions for the designer

- Which parts are actually fitted as IC1 and U1? Check the markings: AP63205 = T5.
- What are C17, C14 and C22 (chemistry, voltage), what are the MOV and F1 ratings, and what are the custom CT suffixes?
- Is serial pin 2 meant to be +12 V or +5 V?
- On the 6-layer board, are the open JP2 pin 2 and the removed C9/C28 intentional?
- Is 277 V a supported input for v6.5.1?
- Is board GND meant to reach PE only through the mounting hardware?
- Is R1 = 820 Ω tuned for a specific IC1 part?

## 5. Reproducing

```bash
KC=/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/MacOS/kicad-cli
python3 docs/research/scripts/eagle_bom.py OpenEVSE_PLUS_v6.5.1/OpenEVSE_PLUS_v6.5.1.sch > bom.csv
python3 docs/research/scripts/netcmp.py eagle_brd OpenEVSE_PLUS_v6.5.1/OpenEVSE_PLUS_v6.5.1.brd \
  kicad_pcb OpenEVSE_PLUS_v6.5.1_6Layer/v6_6layer.kicad_pcb
# Work on copies: DRC with --refill-zones rewrites zone fills, and the import writes a new board.
$KC pcb import --format eagle -o /tmp/x/v6.5.1_2layer.kicad_pcb OpenEVSE_PLUS_v6.5.1/OpenEVSE_PLUS_v6.5.1.brd
cp docs/research/scripts/mains-selv-report.kicad_dru /tmp/x/v6.5.1_2layer.kicad_dru   # needs a .kicad_pro beside it
$KC pcb drc --refill-zones --severity-error --format json -o hv.json /tmp/x/v6.5.1_2layer.kicad_pcb
python3 docs/research/scripts/pilot_sim.py <ti_models_dir> <work_dir> 12 11.7
```

Import note: if KiCad reports items on undefined layers, remap `(layer "UNDEFINED")` to a user layer such as `Cmts.User` before running DRC.
